Trust & privacy

Ask for less. Explain more.

Leka’s beta is manual-entry-first. The core experience can be used without connecting a bank, mobile-money wallet, email inbox, or social account.

Users stay in control

Only provide information needed for the feature you choose to use.

No automatic money movement

The current beta does not move money from your accounts.

Manual-first by design

Budget, log spending, create goals, and receive coaching without account integrations.

Integrations come responsibly

Future integrations should be useful, secure, and clearly explained before use.
